These ratios are for one 9x13 deep dish pan.
## Ingredients 4hr proof 🕧 74% hydration 💧

- 342g of bread flour
- 10g of salt
- 4.5g active dry yeast
- 3253g of luke warm water
- 21g of olive oil

## Steps
## Proccessing Same Day ⌛

1. Mix the dry ingredients in a mixing bowl
2. Add the wet ingredients and mix in with a fork
3. continue to mix until the dough is shaggy and loosly formed
4. Knead for ~5 but no longer than 10 minutes using a mixer fitted with a dough hook
5. keep the dough back in the bowl to proof at room temp.

## Final Prep 💪

- Start preping the oven
    - aim for 500 degrees (ooni or standard oven)
- prepare the 9x13 pan coating lightly with olive oil
- Place the dough into the pan and stretch to the edges as best you can
    - It will spring back alot!
- Cover with with parchment or plastic wrap and a towel
- Check in after ~20 minutes and strecth again
    - It should be less springy now
- Cover it again and let is proof for another ~45m - 1hr

## Make Pizza 🎉🍕
- Make thress stripes of sauce vertuically keeping even distance
    - left, center, right. 
- Cover with shreded moz, parm, provolon mix
    - Take cheese to the edged to get that crispy goodness.
- Add topping of choice.

## Bake Pizza
- Standard oven should be on the lowest rack
- total back time is about 15 minutes
    - Standard oven rotate 180 degrees halfway through
    - Ooni you'll need to keep an eye on it.
        - rotate frequently
        - adjust the temp accordingly
- Remove when done and rest for a few minutes before cutting into it.
