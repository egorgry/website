---
title: Weekend Pancakes
tags: breakfast, sweet
servings: 4
time: 20 min
---

Simple buttermilk pancakes, no resting time required.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 cups flour
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 1.25 cups buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 3 tbsp melted butter

## Instructions

1. Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir until just combined — a few lumps are fine.
4. Cook 1/4 cup portions on a hot, lightly greased griddle until bubbles form, then flip.
5. Cook another 1-2 minutes until golden.
