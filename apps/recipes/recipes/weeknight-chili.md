A no-fuss chili that freezes well. Double the batch if you're freezing half.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

### For the sauce
- 1 can crushed tomatoes (28 oz)
- 1 can kidney beans, drained
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Brown the beef in a large pot over medium-high heat, about 6 minutes.
2. Add the onion and garlic, cook until soft, about 4 minutes.
3. Stir in the sauce ingredients and bring to a simmer.
4. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered for **25 minutes**, stirring occasionally.
5. Taste and adjust salt before serving.

## Notes

Freezes well for up to 3 months. Reheat gently on the stovetop rather than
the microwave to keep the texture right.

- Great over rice or with cornbread
- Leftover chili makes excellent nachos
