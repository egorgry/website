This will make 4 dough balls ~270g 
## Ingredients 4hr proof 🕧 60% hydration 💧

- 663g of 00 flour
- 20g of salt
- 4g active dry yeast
- 398g of luke warm water
## Ingredients 12 hr proof 🕧 65% hydration 💧
- 651 of 00 flour
- 20g of salt
- 1g active dry yeast
- 410g of luke warm water


## Steps
## Proccessing Same Day ⌛

1. Mix the dry ingredients in a mixing bowl
2. Add the water mix in with a fork
3. continue to mix until the dough is shaggy and loosly formed
4. Knead for ~10 minutes using a mixer fitted with a dough hook
5. remove dough and knead by hand until a smooth dough ball is formed
6. Place the dough back in the bowl to proof at room temp.

## Proccessing 24 hour ⏳

1. Follow steps 1 - 5 above
2. Place the dough back in the bowl
3. Cold proof 22-24 hour
4. Proof at room temp for at least 2 hours

## Final Prep 💪

- divide into 4 balls ~270g each
- Form into smooth balls
- Flour a counter or large cutting board
- Place the dough on the floured surface and flour again
- cove with parchment or wax paper and a towel
- Let the dough just about double in size ~45m - 1hr
